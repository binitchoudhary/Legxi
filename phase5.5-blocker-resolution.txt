================================================================================
  ROOT CAUSE NOTE — confirmedDirect bug in ground-truth.mjs
================================================================================

The WRONG_PRODUCT classification for #1318, #1411, #1420, #1446 stems from a
limitation in ground-truth.mjs: the confirmedDirect field is built by matching
line item titles against the CURRENT Shopify product title only. Old-title
purchases (where the line item title = pre-rename product title = tab name) are
not captured in confirmedDirect.

This does not affect the correctness of Phase 2 or Phase 4 for WORKSHEETED rows
(Phase 2 uses its own oldNameDirect check). It only affects the extraInNote
classification for note lines that correspond to confirmed but un-worksheeted
purchases.

Impact: limited to EXTRA_FLAG classification. Since EXTRA_FLAG lines are kept
as-is and not removed, the sync behaviour is already correct for these orders.
A fix to ground-truth.mjs would reclassify these from EXTRA_FLAG (WRONG_PRODUCT)
to EXTRA_KEEP (UNTRACKED_CERT), but this does not change what the sync does.
